Brand refresh · Concept 01

Where downtown slows down.

A facelift for Sierra St. Kitchen & Cocktails. The name, the room and the dates all stay. What changes is a look and a voice that finally match the check.

For Colby Johnson & Megan HollandBy Your StudioDate September 2026
01

The shift: same room, sharper signal.

Sierra St. already has the substance of a premium room: a 35-label cellar, house infusions and plates that reviewers call the best food of their trip. The signals around it still say “cheap bar.” This refresh changes the signals and leaves the substance alone.

“Best Bar in Reno”→Downtown’s riverside small-plates & cocktail room
Happy Hour Daily 04:00 PM – 05:30 PM→Golden Hour: a ritual, not a discount
“Diverse Menu, Something for Everyone”→Something specific, for someone specific
Private party packages→Take the room: Long Table, Winemaker Dinners, Buyouts
Six versions of the name online→One name: Sierra St. Kitchen & Cocktails
Template copy and Lorem ipsum→A house voice, and their own photography

02

Logo suite: the name, set properly.

No new name and no mascot. The wordmark sets “Sierra St.” in spaced Cormorant capitals, keeping the abbreviation guests already use, with the full descriptor underneath. The italic ampersand stands for small plates and cocktails, and it doubles as the icon.

04

Type: a serif with manners, a sans with a spine.

Cormorant Garamond carries the voice: headlines, dish names, the italic accents. Jost handles labels and body copy; its geometric, Futura-like shapes nod to mid-century Reno signage. Both are open-source (SIL OFL), so there are no font licensing fees, ever.

Headline in Cormorant
Body copy in Jost at 17px, 1.65 line height. Comfortable on a phone at the table, and clear on a printed menu in low light.

Numerals: old-style figures in running text (“since 2016”), lining figures for prices and hours, so a $34 entrée reads cleanly.

05

Voice: the bartender who remembers you.

Every “before” below is copied word for word from their current website or social feed.

Specific beats superlative.

Name the dish, the bottle, the street. Never “best,” never “something for everyone.”

Warm, not cute.

Short sentences, plain words, no exclamation points and no hashtags mid-sentence.

Say the price out loud.

Publishing prices, policies and hours plainly is what confidence looks like. A premium room doesn’t hide the check.

06

Illustration & motion: drawn from the menu.

One drawing style: fine lines, real ingredients, and color only where the drink or dish actually has it. It fills the site before the photography exists and keeps working afterwards on menus, coasters and social.

The bar · one glass per signature

Nine drinks, nine silhouettes

Motion rules: slow and ambient. Bubbles rise, smoke drifts, and Purple Rain blooms from blue to violet when tapped. Everything respects reduced-motion settings.

The kitchen · top-down plates

Hover a dish, see the plate

The hero animation, “river lights,” replaces the background video: the Sierra in silhouette, the far bank lit, and reflections on the water. It’s generated in code, so it loads instantly.

09

Rollout: cheapest fixes first.

The first phase costs almost nothing and removes the biggest trust problems. Each phase stands on its own, so the owners can pause between them.

Weeks 1–2

Stop the leaks

  • One set of hours on Google, Yelp, OpenTable and Apple Maps
  • One name everywhere; merge or retire the second Facebook page and the old Wix site
  • Update OpenTable: price band, chef, description
  • Replace the Lorem ipsum About page
Weeks 3–6

Site & menus

  • Launch the new site on the existing booking and ordering tools
  • Menu: every dish named, spelled and priced
  • Publish the cellar list
  • Events packages with a single enquiry form
Weeks 6–10

The room

  • Proper glassware for the signatures
  • Printed menus and cellar list
  • Coasters, napkins, check card
  • Front-window vinyl
Ongoing

Tell it

  • Photography half-day
  • Instagram, led by the tableside Purple Rain
  • The River Letter, monthly
  • Launch Curtain Call and Cellar Night

Scope note. Everything here is a proposal for discussion. Hours, prices, capacities and new programs (Curtain Call, Cellar Night) are placeholders until ownership confirms them.
