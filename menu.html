Large parties. Parties of eight or more include a 20% service charge, which covers gratuity in full. There’s no second tip line.

Allergies. Please tell your server about any allergies. Most plates can be adapted. GF: gluten-free · V: vegetarian.

Details that justify the price

Before “Brussel Sprouts,” “Steak Dianne,” “artisian,” “paired with with”; a filet dish with no name; about half the plates unpriced; no dietary marks.

After Every plate named, spelled and priced (a dash marks prices not published online; fill in before launch). The service-charge policy is stated up front, since reviewers were caught off guard by an automatic 20% plus a tip line. (Limiting it to large parties is our recommendation, not their current policy.) GF/V marks are placeholders for the kitchen to confirm.

Golden Hour

Selected small plates, wines by the glass and house cocktails at gentler prices, every day while the light’s still on the river. First come, first seated.
