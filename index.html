Positioning

Before Page title “Best Bar in Reno.” Headline “Well-Planned Evenings Start Here,” which appears word-for-word on the sites of a sports grill in Indianapolis and a taco shop in Roswell.

After A line only this room can say. “Slows down” sets Sierra St. apart from the casino buzz a block over, and unhurried is what premium feels like. The river-light animation replaces the background video.

10years on
Sierra Street

Est. 2016 · New chapter 2025

A small room by the river where the plates are made for sharing, the cocktails are built by hand, and nobody hurries you out the door. Ten years in, with its longtime manager now an owner, it’s the same room, only more so.

i.

Small plates, done properly

Seared scallops on parmesan risotto. Tuna with cucumber-sake sorbet. The bacon-wrapped dates people drive across town for.

ii.

Cocktails with a point of view

House infusions, from butterfly pea to jalapeño and basil, shaken and stirred to order. Or name a spirit and let the bar decide.

iii.

A cellar that surprises

Thirty-five labels deep, from Willamette Pinot to Barolo. The big Napa names, plus the bottles worth discovering.

Our Story

Same room.
New chapter.

Sierra St. opened in May 2016 on a simple idea: fresh small plates, hand-crafted cocktails, intriguing wines and memorable service, on the ground floor of the Palladio beside the Truckee.

In April 2025, longtime manager Colby Johnson and business partner Megan Holland took it over. They kept the staff and the favorites (yes, the dates), and they’re steering the kitchen toward seasonal, local ingredients.

The best story they aren’t telling

Before The About page reads, in full, “Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet.” OpenTable still lists the previous owner as executive chef.

After The handover story, told plainly: the longtime manager becomes an owner. It also draws a line under older reviews from the previous ownership. Copy is a draft for the owners to edit.
