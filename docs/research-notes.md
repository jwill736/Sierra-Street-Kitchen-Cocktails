## Review themes (older reviews included for context)

- Positive: bacon-wrapped dates (most-mentioned dish), scallops, tuna, cocktails "creative and balanced", friendly named staff, "hidden gem", "quaint and romantic".
- Negative (mostly Valentine's weekend 2025 and earlier, i.e. before the April 2025 sale): automatic 20% service charge plus a tip line, "super expensive", cold food, tables too close, plastic cups, holiday price markups, a reservation honored online but the restaurant closed (Super Bowl Sunday), defensive owner replies (a 2021 review names the previous owner).
- Traffic signal: "I made an early reservation on a Wednesday evening and was the only person here" (June 2026).
